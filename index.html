A letter of gratitude

Dear Sir Jansen De Silva,

On behalf of BSIT IV - Ren[AI]ssance (NOVA), I would like to send our warmest and sincerest thanks. Despite grieving for the loss of your dearest lola, you were able to provide a talk for the seminar that the BSIT students would cherish and use for the years to come. Although it was cut short due to time constraint — it was, to say the least, impactful and very engaging.

The moment I started looking for resource speakers, my friends from the Engineering department immediately pointed me toward you. I looked you up on Facebook to see your background for myself, and your caliber is truly impressive. We are beyond lucky to have you, Sir Jansen, as one of our guest speakers.

I don’t think saying thank you would begin to cover the gratitude that we have for you Sir! We appreciate the dedication and passion that you put in, especially for being an educator. Despite juggling 4 jobs, you still have the kind of smile that looks as if everything you’ve done thus far is all worth it. I hope you will continue to inspire and shine your light on most of us Sir Jansen!

✦ Kaya matsalab malala Sir Jansen ✦

Arym Sherwin Rodas Vendiola

Project Head, Ren[AI]ssance (NOVA)
